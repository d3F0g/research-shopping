## Live verification predicate

A normal request returns real product discoveries, source-linked specifications and any observed price text, or an explicit unavailable/no-evidence result. Product identity and each displayed fact are attributable to safe returned source URLs and extraction evidence. Observation time is not page freshness or merchant confirmation. Unknown stock stays unknown. Stop/reset/edit/disconnect cancels actual provider work. Bounded calls and server-only secrets are proven through the project runtime. Merchant verification remains an unchecked criterion without authorized merchant evidence.
