# Evidence, images, and uncertainty

Recommendations render within assistant messages in `src/client/ResearchAnswer.tsx`. Open **Evidence and tradeoffs for [product]** on the intended message. Require literal supporting quotation, source identity, assessment status and scope, rationale, and unresolved or adverse tradeoffs. AI interpretation remains distinct from quotation and merchant verification.

Open **Sources and research limits**. Compare source destinations with the retained snapshot. Cut source text cannot establish complete support merely because the retained prefix is positive. Conflicts, conditions, negatives, and variant qualifications must survive normalization. Provider boundary tests carry these adversarial cases; a supported fixture card does not cover them.

Inspect observed prices for explicit currency, both budget bounds, exact variant, and complete conditions. Source evidence does not prove inventory, delivery, an authorized merchant offer, or a current price. An unresolved alternative can be useful only with prominent blockers. A free-form summary cannot override server eligibility.

For images, require a retained safe source image URL and visible attribution or representative-image caveat. The helper intercepts exact synthetic image URLs with a labeled local SVG and separately aborts them at short-mobile size. That proves rendering and failure behavior only. Missing or failed images must leave product text and safe source links useful.

Use touch and keyboard to open disclosure controls at mobile size. Capture readable quotations without body-level overflow. Do not count a hover preview as the only accessible citation route. Do not navigate external sources merely to finish an offline check.
