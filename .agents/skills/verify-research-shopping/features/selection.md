# Product and source links

V2 ends at a safe source or merchant product link. There is no separate `/api/select` handoff in the current application. Inspect **View product source** within the intended assistant response.

Require its complete destination to equal a retained safe source URL. Require HTTPS without credentials or an unsupported port, plus `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Inspect server URL-policy and provider tests for private, reserved, malformed, and unsafe destinations. A domain name alone cannot establish manufacturer identity or merchant authorization.

Retained historical links remain evidence navigation, not authority to research or purchase under old preferences. Viewing old sources must not mutate accepted intent or dispatch new paid work. A safe URL does not establish reachability, exact variant, price, inventory, or delivery.

Keep source and price uncertainty visible. Do not place orders, enter checkout, or add merchant access that the application does not have. Fixture manufacturer references retain their historical exact-link policy in tests that still compose them.
